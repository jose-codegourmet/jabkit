# Luxury: reference review

Reviewed 2026-09-14. Source grouping: [TO_EXTRACT.md](../../TO_EXTRACT.md). Desktop browser captures used a 1360 × 900 viewport, supplemented by readable page content. This is a scoped art-direction review, not an accessibility, performance, or full interaction audit. Several sites use entry sequences or virtual scrolling; opening screenshots do not prove downstream behavior.

The proposed system and tokens are original recommendations. The takeaways below are interpretations, not the reference owners' stated design rules.

## [Eleven Bar](https://www.11elevenbar.com)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** An immersive bar interior fills the opening. A horizontal set of nightlife photographs surrounds the central identity, with a reservation control close to the bottom of the view. Warm reflective surfaces and social photography create atmosphere.

**Structure:** The readable page contains an origin story with a skip control, events, cocktail menu, reservations, and contact.

**JabKit interpretation:** Hospitality luxury can feel social and energetic while keeping service actions clear.

**Limit:** Opening imagery and page text were reviewed; audio, reservation flow, and the entire long origin sequence were not tested.

## [Rendez-vous Francéclat](https://rendez-vous.franceclat.fr/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** The opening is a vivid orange-to-red/pink composition divided into vertical fields, with black graphic typography, distributed navigation, event details, and a pass action.

**Structure:** The document introduces the event, then scenographic spaces, makers and brands, imagery, conversations, and practical information.

**JabKit interpretation:** Contemporary cultural luxury can use strong color and graphic structure instead of familiar gold-and-black cues.

**Limit:** Desktop opening and an introduction region were inspected. The full exhibitor collection and interactive panels were not audited.

## [Field Studies Flora](https://www.fieldstudiesflora.com/)

**Evidence:** Opening visual and navigation review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A large botanical arrangement in metal vessels occupies an olive-toned photographic field. A centered wordmark and small serif navigation down the left give it the character of an atelier or archive.

**Structure:** The visible navigation groups services, store, press, contact, seasonal journal, and index.

**JabKit interpretation:** Material-specific imagery can carry the sense of care and value, with navigation acting as a quiet catalogue.

**Limit:** Only the opening and its route labels were inspected. Low-contrast navigation in the captured frame is not a pattern to reproduce.
