# Minimal: reference review

Reviewed 2026-09-14. Source grouping: [TO_EXTRACT.md](../../TO_EXTRACT.md). Desktop browser captures used a 1360 × 900 viewport, supplemented by readable page content. This is a scoped art-direction review, not an accessibility, performance, or full interaction audit. Several sites use entry sequences or virtual scrolling; opening screenshots do not prove downstream behavior.

The proposed system and tokens are original recommendations. The takeaways below are interpretations, not the reference owners' stated design rules.

## [Ewa / Thomas Studio](https://ewathomas.studio/)

**Evidence:** Partial visual review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A pale open canvas, compact stacked wordmark at upper left, a small menu at upper right, and one tall interior-detail photograph offset to the left. The wood, stone, and trailing plant supply almost all the color.

**Structure:** The captured opening behaves as a single gallery view; readable navigation includes about, contact, and shop.

**JabKit interpretation:** A restrained interface can let material detail become the identity.

**Limit:** The document stayed viewport-height in this session; gallery navigation and deeper destinations were not reviewed.

## [ET Studio](https://e-t.studio/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review, with overlay.

**Observed feel and composition:** An oversized wordmark mixes serif and sans forms on a pale field. A later frame shows a large ET mark, widely spaced navigation, and three separated landscape work thumbnails.

**Structure:** Studio, work, and contact form the main route structure; the readable content introduces the practice and its approach.

**JabKit interpretation:** A minimal interface can have an expressive typographic signature and colorful project images.

**Limit:** A consent banner covered the lower viewport. Captures show different loading/entry moments, not a measured animation sequence.

## [Ira June](https://www.irajune.com/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A compact navigation sits above an image-led opening with unequal black-and-white photographs. Lower on the page, small portrait images accompany plainly aligned service options and outlined inquiry controls.

**Structure:** Portfolio, cooperation types, service scopes, about, and contact make a practical progression from work to booking.

**JabKit interpretation:** Minimal does not mean omitting commercial detail; structure can make scope comparisons feel light.

**Limit:** Desktop opening and a service region were inspected; mobile and interactive form behavior were not audited.
