# Neo-brutalism: reference review

Reviewed 2026-09-14. Source grouping: [TO_EXTRACT.md](../../TO_EXTRACT.md). Desktop browser captures used a 1360 × 900 viewport, supplemented by readable page content. This is a scoped art-direction review, not an accessibility, performance, or full interaction audit. Several sites use entry sequences or virtual scrolling; opening screenshots do not prove downstream behavior.

The proposed system and tokens are original recommendations. The takeaways below are interpretations, not the reference owners' stated design rules.

## [Wanted For Nothing](https://wantedfornothing.com/)

**Evidence:** Partial visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** The captured entry uses a dark field, enormous edge-positioned lettering, and a small utilitarian identity block. A later frame introduces centered text while the identity remains visible.

**Structure:** Readable content includes work, services, clients, team/brand material, and an inquiry form.

**JabKit interpretation:** This is a stark typographic interpretation of the group, not evidence that neo-brutalism always requires bright cards.

**Limit:** Entry text appeared blurred during capture. The virtual-scroll page was not fully navigated; its final motion behavior is not claimed.

## [Roze Bunker](https://rozebunker.nl/)

**Evidence:** Limited entry frame plus readable web content.

**Observed feel and composition:** A visible entry frame contains a heavy dark perimeter, pale ground, and a playful yellow fruit mark. A later browser attempt encountered a connection-security screen.

**Structure:** Web-readable content exposes mission, flavors, business, shop, and contact routes, plus an announcement about the Fruitslagers shop.

**JabKit interpretation:** The observed brand mark supports a bold, playful direction. Use product-story structure as a prompt for original composition.

**Limit:** Only the entry graphic was visually verified. Falling-bottle wording was readable, but the interaction itself was not tested.

## [Curry Cafe](https://curry.cafe/)

**Evidence:** Insufficient current evidence.

**Observed feel and composition:** Browser navigation timed out; web retrieval exposed only an image link associated with Facebook.

**Structure:** No usable current page structure was verified.

**JabKit interpretation:** Keep the reference in the list without treating remembered historical designs as current evidence.

**Limit:** No colors, type, composition, or motion are attributed here.

## [Bruno Tomé](https://brunotome.dev/)

**Evidence:** Opening visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** Very large, unusual letterforms occupy a black-and-white modular composition. The image reads as a typographic object before it reads as a conventional portfolio introduction.

**Structure:** Readable content includes repeated career-period text, an informal about statement, social destinations, and contact.

**JabKit interpretation:** Scale and type can carry the visual attitude without a colorful UI palette.

**Limit:** Custom lettering is visible, but the typeface and full navigation behavior were not audited. JabKit should preserve legibility rather than copy the unusual glyphs.

## [Lydia Amaruch](https://lydiaamaruch.com/)

**Evidence:** Opening visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** Oversized condensed filled and outlined role words sit on a dark, visibly divided grid. A small playful signature and smiley motif contrast with the heavy lettering.

**Structure:** Home, work, about, contact, and a pitch-video action appear in the opening content.

**JabKit interpretation:** A disciplined grid can support both forceful typography and personal character.

**Limit:** The frame was captured during entry and looked dim; this is not a final contrast audit. Pitch playback and work routes were not reviewed.
