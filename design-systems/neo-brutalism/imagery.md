# Neo-brutalism: image direction and AI prompts

## Intended feeling

Confident, graphic, slightly mischievous. The asset should read as a strong silhouette at a glance and still reward a closer look.

Hard side light or direct flash, crisp cutouts, chunky recognizable forms, little atmospheric haze. Favor one flat backdrop, one object, and a decisive crop. Natural imperfections are welcome; synthetic dirt is optional and rarely necessary.

This guide is original art direction derived from the system's principles. It does not ask an image model to copy a reference site's layout, logo, or proprietary imagery. The [reference review](references.md) explains what was actually observed.

## Build a prompt from decisions

Use this structure, replacing each bracketed field with a concrete creative decision:

> [Object or campaign subject], [strong silhouette and pose], hard [light direction], [limited palette], [flat background or transparent cutout], graphic commercial photography, [clear area for HTML], [ratio], no typography or logos.

Describe the asset, not an entire finished webpage. Name the subject, intended page role, viewpoint, light, materials, palette, negative space, crop, and exclusions. Request one image per role. HTML owns headlines, body text, prices, navigation, labels, and controls.

## Complete sample prompts

These prompts are for fictional projects and concept assets. They are ready to adapt to a real brief; no output files have been generated by this documentation change.

### Hero / 4:3

> Graphic studio photograph of three unbranded reusable drink bottles standing in a deliberately uneven row, sharply defined silhouettes, dark caps, vivid lime bodies, hard flash from upper left casting distinct shadows to lower right, a flat pale backdrop, bottles grouped on the right half and open space on the left, energetic independent beverage campaign, 4:3 landscape, realistic materials, no text, no logos, no stickers with lettering.

### Cutout / 1:1

> One sculptural red pepper bent into an expressive upright curve, glossy real skin with small natural imperfections, direct studio flash, simple front three-quarter angle, complete silhouette with no cropped tips, isolated transparent background, playful campaign-object photograph, 1:1 square, no words, no face, no logo, no attached shadow outside the silhouette.

### Project image / 3:2

> Overhead photograph of blank lime packaging sleeves and dark rectangular cartons arranged on a pale worktable, offset but aligned into a deliberate graphic composition, hard midday sunlight, crisp shadows, tactile paper stock, ample visible tabletop between objects, 3:2 landscape, no lettering, no printed brand marks, no website mockup.

## Negative direction

Avoid: Soft luxury haze, bevelled pseudo-buttons, chrome blobs, excessive gradients, tiny floating labels, random hazard stripes, repeated smiley stickers, and illegible baked-in display lettering.

Do not merely append “premium,” “beautiful,” or “award-winning.” Replace those words with visible decisions about scale, lighting, material, and composition. If the subject is wrong, revise the subject before adding visual effects.

## Crop, theme, and series consistency

Protect the full object silhouette with 10% clear margin on cutouts. Supply a separate centered phone composition when the desktop uses a large off-center object; keep decorative assets away from the CTA.

A monochrome variant uses high-contrast documentary or studio photography instead of colorful cutouts. Use one approach as the majority language; reserve an exception for a named campaign.

Lock the light direction, lens/viewpoint family, color treatment, and material language for a series. Vary the distance and subject detail, not the entire art direction. Review both the full-size image and a small catalogue thumbnail. Do not invert photography for dark mode; use deliberate crops or compatible transparent edges. Check cutouts on both the light and dark surfaces for halos and lost shadows.

## Handoff to JabKit

Choose each image's role and aspect ratio before generating. Keep the original high-quality output, then create web-sized derivatives with explicit width and height and appropriate responsive sources. Use a meaningful alt description for informative imagery and empty alt for purely decorative assets. Keep a text fallback when media fails.

Store component media locally under the hosted `/assets/...` convention. Follow [preview and asset documentation](../../docs/previews.md); the current vendor command handles supported remote sources and is not a general AI-image importer. Record provenance in the format supported by the repository rather than inventing a remote URL for a generated file. Do not hotlink reference-site assets in components or mocks.

## Art-direction acceptance

The image must serve its section, preserve a recognizable subject at mobile size, leave the required quiet area, and belong to the same series as its neighbors. Reject attractive images with implausible materials, broken object geometry, unintentional text, inconsistent shadows, or a crop that obstructs the action. The page should still communicate the offer if the image is unavailable.
