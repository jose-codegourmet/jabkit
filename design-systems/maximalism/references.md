# Maximalism: reference review

## Source status

The implementation brief originated in a style sample list from talhaxcodes and was split into [FULL VOLUME epic #547](https://github.com/jose-codegourmet/jabkit/issues/547). No individual reference-site URLs were assigned to this specification in `TO_EXTRACT.md`.

Review date: 2026-09-30.

## Evidence available

The epic provides the client and brand, palette, typography direction, composition, illustration, motion, and accessibility direction, a component starting point for each section, fictional campaign copy, the site map and page copy, demo lineup records, image prompts, and ordered implementation tickets. Those requirements are treated as authored project direction rather than observations copied from an external website.

## Interpretation for JabKit

FULL VOLUME translates maximalism into a working festival site:

- two registers on every page: a loud campaign register and a calm transactional register;
- ink, hot pink, acid lime, cobalt, tangerine, and cream mapped onto semantic tokens;
- Anton for short campaign phrases and Inter with tabular numerals for everything a visitor must read or operate;
- original cut-paper collage bounded to one side of a section or a band, never behind reading text;
- kinetic accents with a still version and a persistent pause control;
- schedule, ticket, and policy information in semantic text and controls, with no fake scarcity.

This direction is not a claim that maximalism has one official web component library, palette, or motion system. It is an original JabKit application of layered, high-color composition to the supplied festival brief.

## Limitations

No external reference was inspected for exact CSS, font licensing, animation timing, or responsive behavior. The proposed values in this folder are authored recommendations; the hex palette is the brief's proposed brand values. Anton and Inter must be self-hosted with their licenses during implementation. Final accessibility and visual quality depend on the rendered app, approved imagery, real content length, browser behavior, and both color modes.
