# Editorial: reference review

Reviewed 2026-09-14. Source grouping: [TO_EXTRACT.md](../../TO_EXTRACT.md). Desktop browser captures used a 1360 × 900 viewport, supplemented by readable page content. This is a scoped art-direction review, not an accessibility, performance, or full interaction audit. Several sites use entry sequences or virtual scrolling; opening screenshots do not prove downstream behavior.

The proposed system and tokens are original recommendations. The takeaways below are interpretations, not the reference owners' stated design rules.

## [April Leihuanani](https://aprilleihuanani.com/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review after entry settled.

**Observed feel and composition:** A central portrait is assembled with torn paper, a pin, tape, and travel ephemera on a dark background. A tall serif name treatment, smaller practice line, and understated link complete the opening.

**Structure:** Navigation identifies about, services, work, and contact. Readable sections separate marketing strategy from content creation.

**JabKit interpretation:** Personal material can introduce the author before a structured account of the services.

**Limit:** The initial capture was a loader state; the later settled frame is the basis of the description. Deeper virtual-scroll choreography was not audited.

## [Britt Taylor](https://www.btay.ca/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** Large cream sans-serif statements on charcoal are interrupted by pink emphasis and soft, colorful three-dimensional letter/object imagery. The sequence continues the argument at similarly large scale.

**Structure:** A rhetorical opening develops into a promise about crafted websites, then a contact form.

**JabKit interpretation:** Editorial can be typographic and playful without a serif or magazine grid.

**Limit:** This current page is primarily a short narrative pitch, not a multi-article publication. Mobile behavior was not reviewed.

## [The Sapphic Bar Index](https://sapphicindex.com/)

**Evidence:** Opening visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A paper-colored cover combines a heavy condensed title, compact issue metadata, ticket-like fragments, and a tilted photograph of a drink. A strong dark action anchors the introduction.

**Structure:** The readable document progresses to a ranked index with venue descriptions, locations, notes, and contribution-related content.

**JabKit interpretation:** A publication identity and consistent entry metadata make a collection feel authored.

**Limit:** The opening stayed visible during programmatic scrolling; the index structure was read from the document rather than visually audited throughout. Ratings are the source author’s claims, not verified here.

## [Sarah and Matt](https://sarahandmatt.wedding/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** The opening resembles printed wedding stationery with a blush field, large flowing type, ornamental motifs, and a prominent RSVP route. A later region pairs a chronology with personal photography.

**Structure:** Invitation, relationship timeline, celebration details, travel/stay, registry, and RSVP provide both story and logistics.

**JabKit interpretation:** Personal narrative can coexist with direct practical navigation.

**Limit:** Desktop opening and a timeline/gallery region were inspected. The source’s exact invitation art and personal photographs are not reusable JabKit assets.
