# Retro: reference review

Reviewed 2026-09-14. Source grouping: [TO_EXTRACT.md](../../TO_EXTRACT.md). Desktop browser captures used a 1360 × 900 viewport, supplemented by readable page content. This is a scoped art-direction review, not an accessibility, performance, or full interaction audit. Several sites use entry sequences or virtual scrolling; opening screenshots do not prove downstream behavior.

The proposed system and tokens are original recommendations. The takeaways below are interpretations, not the reference owners' stated design rules.

## [Mat](https://www.matwidget.com/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A warm pale background and deep green rounded lettering surround small inline object cutouts. A cutting-mat-like action treatment and playful collage elements suggest a personal worktable.

**Structure:** Product promise, cutting/arranging/sharing uses, download actions, and a personal maker note form a compact product narrative.

**JabKit interpretation:** The product’s material metaphor can organize both imagery and copy without recreating a vintage operating system.

**Limit:** Desktop opening and maker-note region were inspected; the app itself was not installed or evaluated.

## [Rectangles](https://rectangles.fm/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A dark textured ground holds a large folder-and-paper collage with photographs, notes, and an old computer. Episode imagery resembles handled record sleeves, with contemporary signup controls nearby.

**Structure:** Livestreams, newsletter, takeaways, community, a personal note, and subscription/listening destinations structure the page.

**JabKit interpretation:** A consistent set of analog objects can make different content types feel like one collection.

**Limit:** Opening and episode region were inspected. Playback, signup, and all later content were not audited.

## [Heropack](https://www.heropack.me/)

**Evidence:** Maintenance page.

**Observed feel and composition:** Both browser and web retrieval showed a plain maintenance message on a dark background.

**Structure:** The previous product page was not available.

**JabKit interpretation:** Retain the reference without guessing at its historical art direction.

**Limit:** No style rules are derived from the maintenance screen.

## [Wannathis Retro Devices](https://retro.wannathis.one/)

**Evidence:** Visual and readable-content review.

**Observed feel and composition:** A dark opening pairs large pixel-influenced lettering with brightly colored three-dimensional electronics. A later vivid blue field repeats the device-render language with a computer and keyboard.

**Structure:** The page presents the collection, rendering detail, included items, purchase choices, and a demo download.

**JabKit interpretation:** A coherent camera and material language can make a product collection feel nostalgic and current at once.

**Limit:** The page’s prices and compatibility claims were not evaluated as purchase advice. JabKit’s analog-desk default is a separate proposed direction.
