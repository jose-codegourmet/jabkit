## Pillo brief as the working source

**Evidence:** Readable product brief in epic #536, including the website build brief, page tables, demo-data rules, and `cla-*` asset list.

**Observed feel and composition:** The brief specifies a warm cream canvas `#FFF8F0`, coral `#FF826E`, lavender `#B7A3ED`, mint `#A8DCC4`, butter `#F6D978`, and deep plum `#332B3E`, with Nunito and Nunito Sans, generous radii, and one upper-left light.

**Structure:** Home, How it works, For families, Routine ideas, routine detail, Pricing, FAQ, Create a free board, Contact, Privacy, Terms, and 404. Global header, mobile sheet, CTA band, and footer are specified there.

**JabKit interpretation:** Implement those pages in `apps/claymorphism` with semantic `--jk-*` tokens and JabKit components as starting points. Record client-unconfirmed prices and policies as placeholders.

**Limit:** The brief describes intended product behavior. It is not a shipped production policy, price list, or privacy notice. Items marked for the client stay unconfirmed.
